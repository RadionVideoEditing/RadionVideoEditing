## My editing approach

Good editing is not just cutting out pauses. It helps the viewer understand the idea, follow the story and keep watching.

For expert and documentary content, I focus on:

- a strong opening and clear first impression;
- logical structure and smooth transitions between ideas;
- natural rhythm without over-editing;
- clean voice and well-balanced sound;
- visual support for important points;
- a consistent look from the first frame to the last.
